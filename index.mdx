Archery gives you a Laravel-inspired application structure while staying native to `dart:io`; you will learn where its major features fit and how to start building with them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the package">
    Add Archery to your Dart project and fetch its dependencies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Boot the application">
    Create `App`, load `AppConfig`, register providers, and call `app.boot()`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the HTTP layer">
    Resolve `Router` and `StaticFilesServer`, create `AppKernel`, then pass incoming requests through static serving and the kernel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your features">
    Add routes, views, models, middleware, authentication, sessions, and CSRF protection as your application requires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Archery 1.5.0 describes itself as stable alpha and early production software. Review the deployment guidance before exposing an application publicly.
</Info>
